**Course objectives** 

There are three goals of lectures in history of economic thought:  **1\. To present (describe) and to assess the main economic  theories.**  

**2\. To show that the development of economic thought results  from changes in real economic phenomena.**  

**3\. To show that there is no generally accepted economic theory,  even today. One distinguishes one dominant theory that is an  economic standard or a paradigm.**

**Knowledge** 

• The lecture on history of economic thought allows the students to deepen the  knowledge of economic theory that they acquire during their studies of basics of  economics, microeconomics and macroeconomics. 

• The course allows students to systematize knowledge on economic theory, allows  them to associate conceptions and ideas to separate schools of economic thought as  well as to important social thinkers. 

• The course allows students to recognize fundamental economic problems within  different historically existing economic systems.  
**Skills** 

• Students and graduates gain the ability to ascribe specific  economic opinions, especially those referring to current  events, to concrete economic theories and to assess these  events using appropriate theoretical instruments. 

• The course allows students to ascribe economic views  (including those pertaining to contemporary events) to  concreto economic theories and to evaluate these views in the  light of such theories. 

• The course allows students to evaluate opinions and  pronouncements of scientists and practitioners on economic  subjects. 

• The course allows students to follow and/or to take part in  current economic debates.  
**Social competences** 

• If a graduate decides to work as a scientist  and/or an academic teacher, history of  economic thought will help them to locate  their research activity into a broader scientific  field. 

• The course allows students to properly locate  their own research area in the whole of  economic thought.

**History of Economic Thought**  

• deals with different thinkers and theories in the subject that  became political economy and later economics, from the  ancient world to the present day. It encompasses many  different schools of economic thought.  

• History of economics thought deals with the origin and  development of economic ideas and their interrelations. It is a  historical account of economic doctrines.  
**The history of economic thought can**  

**further be broadly divided into two parts:** 

1\. The Origin and the development of economic ideas before the  development of economics as science. 

2\. The second part deals with economic ideas after the  development of economics as a Science.

**Economics as a social science** 

• Economics is considered a social science because it seeks to explain how  society deals with the scarcity problem, 

• Economics is one of several disciplines that apply the scientific method  to the study of human behavior in social science  
**SCIENTIFIC METHOD** 

**The scientific method** is a body of techniques for investigating phenomena, acquiring new knowledge, or correcting and integrating previous knowledge. To be termed scientific, a method of inquiry is commonly based on empirical or measurable evidence subject to specific principles of reasoning.  
**Components of the Scientific Method** 

• Model, 

• Theory, 

• Principles, 

• World View, 

• Hypotheses, 

• Verification.  
**Other components of the scientific method:** 

• Replication, 

• External review  
**The steps of the scientific method are to:** 

• Ask a Question.(Problem) 

• Do Background Research. (Observation) • Construct a Hypothesis. (Formulate  Hypotheses) 

• Test Your Hypotheses. (By Doing an  Experiment) 

• Analyze your Data .(Analyze Your Results) • Communicate Your Results. (Conclusion)  
**Inductive and Deductive Method of Reasoning:** 

• **Deductive** or **Classical Reasoning Approach**: It works from the more general to the more specific. Sometimes this is informally called a "top-down" approach. 

• **Inductive Reasoning Approach**: The Historical school emphasized on the inductive method. • These economists believe that the laws of economics are not universal in nature.  
**The history of economic thought can be studied and analyzed by  adopting different approaches:** 

1\. Deductive or Classical approach 

2\. Inductive approach 

3\. Chronological approach 

4\. Conceptual approach 

5\. Philosophical approach 

6\. Neo-Classical approach 

7\. Welfare approach 

8\. Keynesian approach 

9\. Institutional approach
